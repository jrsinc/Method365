What we do

Six disciplines. One accountable partner.

Engage us for a single function or an integrated program. Each service is delivered by senior professionals who have done the work inside sponsors and CROs — and who know what regulators and auditors look for.

Clinical Operations

Hands-on operational leadership from study start-up through close-out, so your trial runs to plan, on budget and to ICH-GCP standard.

  • Study start-up and country/site feasibility
  • Risk-based monitoring strategy and CRA oversight
  • CRO and vendor selection, contracting and oversight
  • Trial Master File (TMF) set-up and health checks

Clinical Project Management

Experienced project leads who keep scope, timelines and budgets aligned — and keep every stakeholder informed and accountable.

  • Integrated study timelines and milestone tracking
  • Budget forecasting, accruals and change orders
  • Risk registers, issue escalation and mitigation
  • Sponsor governance meetings and status reporting

Clinical Trial Site Management

Practical, on-the-ground support that helps investigator sites activate faster, enroll steadily and remain inspection-ready.

  • Site selection visits and activation packages
  • IRB/IEC submissions and essential documents
  • Patient recruitment and retention planning
  • Coordinator support, training and ISF maintenance

GxP Consulting Services

Quality and compliance expertise across GCP, GMP, GLP and GDP — building systems and habits that hold up under regulatory scrutiny.

  • Compliance gap assessments against current regulations
  • SOP development, review and training
  • Quality management system design and vendor qualification
  • Deviation, root-cause analysis and CAPA management

Audit Preparation

Walk into sponsor audits and regulatory inspections — FDA, EMA and other authorities — prepared, organized and confident.

  • Mock audits and inspection-readiness programs
  • TMF, ISF and source-document quality review
  • Storyboards and staff interview preparation
  • Finding responses and CAPA follow-through

Clinical Supply Management Consulting

The right investigational product at the right site at the right time — compliant, temperature-controlled and without waste or stock-outs.

  • Demand forecasting and supply strategy
  • Packaging, labeling and global distribution planning
  • IRT/RTSM design, UAT and oversight
  • Cold-chain, excursions, reconciliation and returns
